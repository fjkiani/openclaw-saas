This Co-Founder Services and Restricted Stock Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of [EFFECTIVE DATE] (the "Effective Date") by and between:

Dr. Robin Kim, MD, an individual residing at [ADDRESS] (the "Co-Founder").
The Company and Co-Founder are referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."
1. Purpose
The Company is developing a mechanism-aware oncology platform for clinical trial matching, patient stratification, and related clinical intelligence products (the "Platform"). The Company ecosystem includes product lines such as VetOnco, Longevity, and DeepCrispr.ai (collectively, the "Company Products"). The Company desires to engage Co-Founder as a strategic full-time clinical co-founder and Chief Medical Officer (CMO), and Co-Founder desires to provide such services, subject to the terms of this Agreement.
This Agreement is intended to create a win-win structure: Co-Founder may continue permitted medical, academic, financial, and advisory activities, while the Company receives strong protection for its intellectual property, confidential information, relationships, and core commercial field.
2. Definitions
2.1 Cause
"Cause" means any of the following: (i) material breach of this Agreement or any other written agreement with the Company that remains uncured for 30 days after written notice; (ii) fraud, embezzlement, willful misconduct, or gross negligence that causes material harm to the Company; (iii) conviction of a felony or crime involving dishonesty; (iv) unauthorized use of Confidential Information; (v) material violation of written policies. "Cause" specifically excludes any failure to meet Milestone-linked vesting criteria under Schedule B.
2.2 Change of Control
Standard definition as a merger, consolidation, or reorganization in which stockholders before the transaction hold less than 50% of the voting power after; or a sale of all/substantially all assets.
2.3 Confidential Information
Standard non-public information definition including algorithms, source code, product roadmaps, datasets, model logic, training/validation methods, and technical documentation.
2.4 Good Reason
"Good Reason" means, without Co-Founder's prior written consent: (i) a material reduction in Co-Founder's title, authority, responsibilities, or clinical oversight; (ii) a material breach of this Agreement by the Company; or (iii) a Company directive requiring unlawful conduct.
2.5 Mutual Dependency
"Mutual Dependency" means a condition where a Milestone is delayed or impaired due to a lack of Company-provided technical resources, data access, API availability, or engineering support. In such events, the Milestone shall be deemed "Satisfied" for the purpose of vesting tranches.
2.6 Reserved Company Field
Standard oncology AI trial matching/stratification definition.
2.7 Work Product
Standard works of authorship and inventions definition created in connection with Company Products.
3. Status, Role, and Services
3.1 Co-Founder Status
The Company recognizes Co-Founder as a strategic clinical co-founder.
3.2 Title
Co-Founder shall serve as Chief Medical Officer ("CMO").
3.3 Independent Contractor
Co-Founder provides services as an independent contractor. Co-Founder is solely responsible for all taxes and insurance.
3.4 Services and Commitment
Co-Founder shall provide services on a full-time basis, including clinical strategy, medical validation across all Company Products, clinical partnerships, and translational positioning.
4. Outside Activities; Win-Win Structure
4.1 Permitted Activities
The Company acknowledges that Co-Founder may continue to engage in pre-existing financial and professional endeavors,  those disclosed on Schedule C-1.
4.2 Personal Branding
Co-Founder retains the right to build a personal professional brand on LinkedIn. Co-Founder may identify as "Co-Founder & CMO of CrisPRO.ai" to promote Company visibility while maintaining an individual professional voice and thought leadership.
5. Equity Grant and Compliance
5.1 Grant
Subject to Board approval, the Company shall issue shares representing 25% of the Company's fully diluted capitalization (calculated including the 19% investor/early-team pool and the employee option pool) as set forth on Schedule A. The binding figure shall be the share number. Ownership applies to the parent entity and all present and future subsidiaries (VetOnco, Longevity, DeepCrispr.ai).
5.2 Liability Shield and Insurance
(i) RUO Status: All Platform and Company Product outputs are for Research Use Only (RUO) during this phase.
(ii) Indemnification: The Company shall maintain Directors & Officers (D&O) Insurance.
(iii) Professional Liability: The Company shall maintain a policy covering "AI-assisted clinical decision support."
(iv) Clinical Disclaimer: All external predictions must clarify that AI provides clinical decision support, not diagnosis.
5.3 Vesting Schedule
Initial Vesting: 5% of the Equity Grant shall vest on the Effective Date.
Cliff: No additional shares vest until the 12-month anniversary.
Monthly Vesting: Thereafter, remaining shares vest in equal monthly installments over 36 months.
Milestone-linked Vesting: Tranches under Schedule B are subject to the Mutual Dependency clause.

5.4 Tax & Administrative Compliance
The Restricted Stock Purchase Agreement (RSPA) shall be executed concurrently with this contract. The Board Consent authorizing this grant shall be issued and signed alongside this Agreement. Co-Founder shall file a Section 83(b) election via certified mail within 30 days of the grant.
5.5 Acceleration
Standard single (25%) and double (100%) trigger acceleration as defined in the 3.0 draft.

6.4 Further Assurances

7.4 DTSA Notice

8.4 Non-Solicitation of Personnel

8.5 Non-Solicitation of Counterparties
