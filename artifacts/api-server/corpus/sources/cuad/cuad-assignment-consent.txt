Assignment — Consent Required

Clause Type: assignment
Source: CUAD v1 (CC BY 4.0, Atticus Project) — representative pattern

Neither party may assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of the other party, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed. Notwithstanding the foregoing, either party may assign this Agreement without consent to: (a) an affiliate of the assigning party; or (b) a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of the assigning party's assets, provided that the assignee assumes all obligations under this Agreement.
