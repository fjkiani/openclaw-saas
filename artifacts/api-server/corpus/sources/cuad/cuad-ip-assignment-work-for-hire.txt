IP Assignment — Work for Hire Designation

Clause Type: ip_assignment
Source: CUAD v1 (CC BY 4.0, Atticus Project) — representative pattern

All work product created by Employee within the scope of Employee's employment shall be considered a 'work made for hire' as defined in 17 U.S.C. §101, and the Company shall be the author and owner of all rights in such work product. To the extent any work product is not considered a work made for hire, Employee hereby assigns all right, title, and interest in such work product to the Company, including all copyrights, patent rights, and other intellectual property rights.
