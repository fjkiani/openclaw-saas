IP Assignment — Prior Inventions Carve-Out

Clause Type: ip_assignment
Source: CUAD v1 (CC BY 4.0, Atticus Project) — representative pattern

Employee has listed on Schedule A attached hereto all inventions, ideas, improvements, and discoveries, whether patentable or not, that Employee made or conceived prior to the date of this Agreement and that Employee wishes to exclude from the scope of this Agreement. Employee represents that Schedule A is complete. If no items are listed on Schedule A, Employee represents that Employee has no such prior inventions. The Company acknowledges that Employee retains all right, title, and interest in the items listed on Schedule A.
