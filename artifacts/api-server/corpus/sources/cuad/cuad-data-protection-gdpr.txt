Data Protection — GDPR Compliance

Clause Type: confidentiality
Source: CUAD v1 (CC BY 4.0, Atticus Project) — representative pattern

To the extent that Vendor processes personal data originating from the European Economic Area on behalf of Customer, Vendor shall comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Vendor shall: (a) process personal data only on Customer's documented instructions; (b) ensure that persons authorized to process personal data have committed to confidentiality; (c) implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures; (d) not transfer personal data outside the EEA without appropriate safeguards; and (e) assist Customer in responding to data subject requests and breach notifications.
