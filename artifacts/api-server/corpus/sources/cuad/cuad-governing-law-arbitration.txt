Governing Law — Mandatory Arbitration

Clause Type: governing_law
Source: CUAD v1 (CC BY 4.0, Atticus Project) — representative pattern

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be resolved by binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association under its Commercial Arbitration Rules. The arbitration shall be conducted in Wilmington, Delaware by a single arbitrator. Judgment on the arbitration award may be entered in any court of competent jurisdiction.
