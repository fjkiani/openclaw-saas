IP Assignment — Broad Scope

Clause Type: ip_assignment
Source: CUAD v1 (CC BY 4.0, Atticus Project) — representative pattern

Employee hereby assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to all inventions, improvements, designs, works of authorship, and other intellectual property, whether or not patentable or copyrightable, that Employee makes, conceives, or reduces to practice, whether alone or jointly with others, during the period of Employee's employment, whether or not during working hours or using Company equipment. This assignment applies to all inventions whether or not related to the business of the Company.
