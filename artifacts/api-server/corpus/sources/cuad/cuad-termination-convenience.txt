Termination — For Convenience

Clause Type: termination
Source: CUAD v1 (CC BY 4.0, Atticus Project) — representative pattern

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon ninety (90) days' prior written notice to the other party. In the event of termination for convenience, the terminating party shall pay the non-terminating party for all goods delivered and services performed through the effective date of termination, plus any non-cancelable commitments reasonably incurred prior to the termination notice.
